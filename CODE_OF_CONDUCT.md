# Code of conduct

This project is a place to build a useful tool together.

- Be kind and assume good intent. Criticise ideas and code, not people.
- Keep it about the work. No harassment, insults, sexual language or imagery, and no publishing
  of anyone's private information.
- Remember who uses a cleaner: people who are worried about their PC. Answer questions the way
  you would want a patient expert to answer yours.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and contributions that break these rules, and
may block people who keep breaking them.

To report a problem, contact the maintainers privately through
[a security advisory](https://github.com/SkippyNet/zest/security/advisories/new) (it is only
visible to maintainers) and say that it is a conduct report.
