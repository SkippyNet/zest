# Zest guide

Zest is a free, open-source cleaner and optimizer for Windows 10 and 11. This guide explains how
to install it, what each tool does, and what it will and will not touch.

**New here?** Read [Installing](Installing.md) and then [Your first clean](Your-first-clean.md). That is
ten minutes, and it covers the part most people use.

## Three things to know first

1. **Analyzing changes nothing.** Every tool looks first and shows you a list. Nothing is removed
   or changed until you press the button that says so, and most of those ask once more.
2. **Privacy cleaning is off until you turn it on.** Out of the box Zest removes junk: temporary
   files, caches, logs. Cookies, history, saved passwords and open tabs are left alone unless you
   tick those rules yourself.
3. **Some tools need administrator rights.** They say so, and offer *Restart as administrator*.
   Everything else runs with your normal rights.
